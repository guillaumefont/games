# Vantage

An original vertical shoot 'em up for phones (portrait) that swoops into third-person chases for its final bosses.
Godot 4.7, low poly 3D built entirely in code.

- **Vertical stage:** drag anywhere to fly (1:1), the guns fire on their own. Shoot down fighters,
  ground flak and gunships, and collect the scrap they drop.
- **Mid-boss:** the Bastion, a battleship wider than the screen, fought top-down and taken apart section
  by section: bow guns, wing batteries (each section breaks off), then the bridge.
- **Final boss, the carrier chase:** the camera swoops down behind the ship (no cut, it's the same 3D
  world) and you chase a carrier bigger than the screen, in third person, on rails. Drag to move and
  dodge; the guns fire ahead with a little aim assist. Take it apart in sections: its stern guns, its
  tower, then the reactor in its hangar behind four shield panels, while drones keep launching.
- **Hangar:** scrap buys permanent upgrades (cannon, missiles, hull, magnet) used in both modes.

Desktop: arrows/WASD also fly and aim, Enter launches.
