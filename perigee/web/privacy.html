Perigee privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Perigee is a one-button arcade game for Android and the web, made by an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and the game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your scores, Voyage progress, sparks, unlocked cosmetics and settings are saved on your device only.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send any data to us or to anyone else.
  • It works offline. The only permission it asks for is vibration, for haptic feedback.

What is stored, and where

The game keeps one save file in the app's own storage: your best scores, your Voyage stars and times, the sparks you have collected, the cosmetics you have unlocked and equipped, and your settings (sound, music and effects volume, vibration, aim guide, reduced motion, high-contrast rings). This never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) deletes it. In a browser, the same data is kept in the browser's storage for this site.

Purchases

The game has no purchases yet. If we add them, they will go through Google Play or the App Store, and this page will say what those stores share with us before any purchase is offered.

Children

Perigee is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Sharing and selling

We don't collect your data, so we don't sell or share it.

Your rights

Everything the game knows about you is in the save file on your device, and you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. If you have a question, contact us at the address below.
