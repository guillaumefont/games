Perigee privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Perigee is a one-button arcade game for Android and the web, made by an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and the game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your best score, sound setting and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved on your device only.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob and measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase. Both are Google services and collect device data as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The web version in a browser has no ads, no purchases and no analytics.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your best score, your sound setting and your "Remove ads" status in the app's own storage. This never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free Android app shows an ad between some runs, and offers an optional ad you can watch to continue a run. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads use your data for personalisation, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the game's start screen. Elsewhere, you can opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads), and reset or delete your advertising ID there.

Buying "Remove ads" stops the ads between runs. Google's own use of the data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn whether players come back and which parts of the game work, the Android app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example that a run started or ended, its score, and whether an ad was shown or a purchase made. These events come with an app instance ID (a random number tied to this install), your device model, Android version, language and approximate country (worked out from your IP address, which is not stored). We see these only as totals and trends, not as data about a person.

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics follow the same consent choice as ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and shares with us only what we need to deliver the purchase: the product, an order number and whether it was paid. Google's privacy policy covers your payment data.

Children

Perigee is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone other than the Google services named above, which process it for the purposes described here.

Your rights

Because the game has no accounts, we can't link data to you by name. You can reset your advertising ID, opt out of personalised ads, change your consent with the Privacy button, or uninstall the app to stop all collection. If you have a question or a request about your data, including access or deletion under the GDPR or similar laws, contact us at the address below.
