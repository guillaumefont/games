# Mer des Brumes

An original low poly pirate game of naval battles in Godot 4.7, in portrait for phones. Sail a misty
archipelago, fight broadsides (get behind your enemy: its guns fire to the sides), sink ships for coins and
treasure maps, and spend it all at your port on upgrades, crew, towers and new hulls.

- Play: https://guillaumefont.github.io/games/mer-des-brumes/ (Android APK next to it)
- The web prototype it was ported from: [`web/index.html`](web/index.html), published under `prototype/`.

Controls: put your thumb down anywhere on the sea and slide: the direction is the heading, the distance the
speed (the sail stays set when you lift). Keyboard: Q/D (or A/D, arrows) to turn, Z/S (W/S) for sail.

See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) for how it's built, tested and released.
